Why not 30 PKR per full video?

Full AI video generation can be expensive because video models are billed by output seconds. For example, current provider pricing can reach several cents per second, so VidPak should keep 30 PKR as a future limited/quick tier rather than promise a full unlimited-quality video at that price.
